# PortPass Bahamas logo files: the Prow, Harbour Signal

Made 30 Sept 2026 from the Prow mark chosen on the brand board on 28 Sept. The mark is the same shape as the one on the brand board. The wordmark is PORTPASS in Archivo Expanded Black, with BAHAMAS underneath, converted to outlines so no fonts are needed.

**For the website:** unzip so this folder becomes `public/brand/` in the portpass repo (branch `feat/harbour-signal`, brief 04).

## What's inside
| Folder | Files | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `logo/` | `portpass-logo-horizontal-{light,dark,black,white}.svg` | Site header (light), Night header and footer (dark), one-colour jobs |
| `logo/` | `portpass-logo-stacked-*.svg` | Banners, signage, square spaces |
| `logo/` | `portpass-mark-*.svg`, `portpass-mark-small-*.svg` | The mark on its own. Use the small mark below 32px |
| `logo/` | `portpass-wordmark-*.svg` | Wordmark on its own (rare) |
| `logo/png/` | PNG copies of all of the above | Documents, WhatsApp, anything that can't take SVG |
| `icons/` | `favicon.svg` (switches for dark mode), `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `favicon-16/32/48.png` | Browser tab |
| `icons/` | `apple-touch-icon-180.png`, `app-icon-192.png`, `app-icon-512.png`, `app-icon-maskable-512.png` | Home-screen and app icons (manifest) |
| `social/` | `instagram-profile-1080.png`, `share-image-1200x630.png` | Instagram and WhatsApp profile picture; link previews |
| `print/` | PDFs of the horizontal, stacked and mark versions | **Printers get these (or the SVGs), never the PNGs** |

**Versions:**
- **light:** navy ship, white P, red stripes. Use it on white or light backgrounds.
- **dark:** white ship, navy P, red stripes. Use it on navy or dark photos.
- **black and white:** one colour, with the P and stripes cut out.

## Rules
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the P.
- Minimum sizes:
  - the mark: 16px on screen, 8mm in print;
  - the horizontal logo: 120px wide, 30mm in print.
- Don't stretch, rotate or recolour it, don't add shadows, and don't put it on busy photos.
