# PortPass search and AI visibility

Goal: people searching in The Bahamas (on Google, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) find a PortPass business and book it on PortPass.

Sources: "SEO in the AI era" (Surfer course), the Semrush beginner tutorial, James Dooley with Joy Hawkins, and Charlie Whitworth on Search with Candor. Full explainer: the "Get Found, Get Booked" deck. The project doc `claude/Get_Found_Get_Booked_Playbook_7Oct.md` and `11_SEO_Foundations.md` hold the current plan. Read them first and don't contradict them without saying so.

## Core beliefs
- SEO is the foundation of AI search. ChatGPT searches through Bing's index; Claude and Gemini lean on Google. Being indexed, clear and trusted is what gets a business quoted.
- AI-ready SEO is good SEO done cleaner, not a separate service to sell twice.
- Money pages (bookable listings) come first. Guides and comparisons exist to feed them.
- Honest only: no bought links, upvotes or fake engagement. Paid placement never touches rankings or lists.

## Steps
1. **Intent check.** For each target phrase, name the intent: informational (learn), commercial (compare), transactional (buy) or navigational (brand). Say what page type ranks for it today (Google it if you can browse). Match that page type.
2. **Keyword sweet spot.** Score each phrase on demand, fit, intent and difficulty. Prefer long-tail local phrases ("speaker rental Nassau" over "speakers"). Drop any phrase with no path to a booking.
3. **Build order.** Money pages (live listings) first, across the board. Then ONE cluster at a time, tied to a money page: comparison pieces, then guides, all linking down. Keep the existing rule: category pages stay noindex until 5 live listings.
4. **Write the page.**
   - Research the top 5 results and note what they miss.
   - Outline the headings.
   - Draft. Add what AI can't: real prices, real Bahamian businesses, owner experience, PortPass booking data.
   - Write descriptions in semantic triples that start with the business name: "Treble House Ent. rents speakers in Nassau for $50 for 8 hours." Never open with "we" or "our story".
   - No filler: every paragraph has one job.
5. **On-page checklist.**
   - Title: keyword first, under 60 characters ("[Business]: [what] in [area], Nassau | PortPass").
   - One H1.
   - H2s ordered by the customer's biggest question: price first, or speed for urgent services.
   - A short URL with the keyword.
   - A 150–160 character meta description written like an ad.
   - Internal links from guides and lists down to the listing.
   - Descriptive image file names, alt text and WebP.
   - Structured data per `11_SEO_Foundations.md`.
6. **Trust.**
   - Ask for Google reviews after bookings, one to one, and reply to every review.
   - Earn mentions and honest "best of" lists that give reasons.
   - Be the source: aggregated, anonymous PortPass data.
   - Free tools, e.g. an event budget calculator.
   - Journalist queries (Source of Sources).
   - Judge a link by whether it indexes and ranks, not by DR.
7. **AI readiness.**
   - Pages fast and server-rendered; motion design must not hide text from crawlers.
   - Clear headings.
   - One page per thing: prune or merge duplicates.
   - The same business name everywhere.
   - Bot and firewall settings don't block Google, Bing or AI crawlers.
8. **Traffic and social.**
   - Point paid boosts and business ads at PortPass pages, never separate landing pages.
   - Social posts start with the business name; the first 7–12 words act as the title. No hashtags on LinkedIn posts meant to rank.
9. **Measure.**
   - Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools: impressions, new keywords, position moving from page 3 to page 1.
   - AI referral visits and bookings.
   - A monthly private-window check: ask ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini "who's the best [service] in Nassau?" and note who appears.
   - Don't use "AI visibility scores" as the KPI.

## Tools
- Free first: Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, Google Business Profile, Keyword Surfer, Screaming Frog (free to 500 URLs).
- Paid once they pay off: Semrush (keyword gap "weak" keywords are quick wins, Keyword Magic with intent filter, site audit, position tracking with visibility %) and Surfer (content score).
- Check current prices before recommending.
