# PortPass idea check

Antonio wants Claude to think about the future with him and bring "next big thing" ideas, AND to tell him plainly when a plan doesn't make sense. This skill does both. It never kills the vision; it finds the fastest honest route to it.

Sources: Michael Skok (business models), Guy Kawasaki (The Art of the Start), and the green-products panel (Method, Clorox, IDEO). Full explainer: the "Get Found, Get Booked" deck and `claude/Get_Found_Get_Booked_Playbook_7Oct.md`.

## Steps
1. **Restate the idea** in one sentence: who it is for, what they get, how PortPass makes money.
2. **Meaning (Kawasaki).** Which of the three does it serve: improve quality of life, right a wrong, or stop something good from ending? Does it fit the mantra "Connecting The Bahamas"?
3. **Core, multipliers, levers (Skok).**
   - Does it strengthen the core (booking and paying Bahamian businesses, and the data it creates) or distract from it?
   - Name the multipliers: revenue, reach, channels, partners' stacks.
   - Name the levers: lower cost, co-creation, referrals, web marketing.
   - In partnerships, check who is paid to sell it and who carries the support cost.
4. **Slippery check.** Score it on simple, free or cheap to start, quick setup, fast proof of value, plays well with WhatsApp and Instagram, obvious payback, and "can't live without it". Name the biggest friction.
5. **Russian doll.** Where does it sit in the tiers (free taste → Growing → Business → Pay + Market)? What is the small first step, and what is the upsell?
6. **Unit economics.** Rough life-cycle value ÷ cost to acquire and re-engage. Flag anything under about 3. Show the arithmetic; label assumed numbers as assumptions.
7. **High and to the right.** Is it unique AND valuable to the customer? If others already do it, it's a price war. If nobody wants it, say so.
8. **MAT (Kawasaki).**
   - Which milestone does it move? Current milestones: first paid booking through PortPass, 10 businesses live and taking bookings, first paying plan, 1,000 Google visits a month. Check memory and project docs for newer ones.
   - Which assumptions must be tested first, and how cheaply?
   - If it's a task that moves no milestone yet, say "right for later" and name what has to be true first.
9. **No sacrifice (green panel).** Does it make customers or businesses give anything up? Lead with the experience, not a lecture. If it needs an education campaign, rethink the design. Don't overclaim.
10. **Next big thing.** Offer one bolder version of the idea that keeps the core, and one cheaper first test.

## Output
- One-line verdict: go now / go after [milestone] / rethink.
- Then short sections for steps 3–9, then the bold version and the cheap test.
- If Antonio wants to understand or present it, make a short slides deck in the PortPass navy/red palette with a core/multipliers/levers diagram and the 2x2.
- Be honest and kind. Never invent statistics.
